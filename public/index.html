AN OPEN ATLAS. AN HONEST ANSWER.

Know what’s behind
your next journey.

01

Stations & trains

Indian station coordinates and train routes come from the MIT-licensed Indian Railway Data community dataset. The original timetable’s effective date is unknown. Running days, times and train numbers must be checked with NTES or IRCTC. Seat availability is not provided.

02

Airports & connections

Airport locations and scheduled-service flags: OurAirports, public domain. Connections: AirportMiles, including DGCA India traffic statistics and source-specific attribution. A reported city pair is not a promise of a current nonstop flight. Traffic combines both directions; separate outbound and inbound schedules are unavailable.

03

Cost & convenience

₹ ranges are modelled planning budgets per person, not bookable quotes. Train models: sleeper ₹0.55–0.85/km + ₹40–80; AC ₹1.25–1.80/km + ₹40–80; chair ₹0.70–1.20/km + ₹40–80, with minimums. Domestic air: ₹1,500–3,500 + ₹2.50–5/km per flight. International air: ₹3,500–7,000 + ₹3.20–6/km. Cab fares are shared by party size; tickets are per traveller. Flights include 2½ hours for check-in/exit; a connection adds 3 hours. Train transfers require at least 90 minutes. Connection times are planning assumptions.

04

Maps & your privacy

Maps and explicit online place searches use OpenStreetMap. Local suggestions require no network calls. Nominatim is called only when you select “Search online”, cached for 7 days and limited to at most one request every 1.2 seconds. This free public service is for light use, has no SLA and may block high traffic; switch providers for a public launch at scale. Outside India, nearby rail discovery uses Overpass on request. Map and service providers receive requests; precise location is requested only when you tap “Use my location”. We run no tracking. Saved trips stay in your browser until removed.

05

What “best” means

Best balance adds the middle of the estimated fare range to total hours × your chosen value of time, plus ₹600 per change. Fewest changes penalises transfers and extra legs. Lower carbon uses illustrative factors: train 25 g, flight 150 g and road 120 g CO₂ per passenger-km. These are broad mode comparisons, not audited emissions. Hubs are ranked by their presence in the bundled rail and air networks.

06

Coverage & refresh

Reading data manifest…

Airport route data belongs to a validated snapshot. Use Refresh data on the local server to download newer sources; use Data status to see download dates and reporting periods. Foreign airport connections load on demand where available. Train route lines connect reported stops; flight lines connect airports. Road lines are schematic. Costs exclude meals, hotels, visas and checked luggage. Separate tickets have no guaranteed connection protection.
